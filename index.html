AI Video Prompt Linter

Paste a prompt for any AI video model. The linter flags the mistakes that most often waste a generation, before you press render.

Paste a prompt and press Lint.

What it checks

  • Camera: none stated, or more than one move.
  • Preserve clause: nothing saying what must stay the same.
  • Exclusions: no "no text / no extra people".
  • Filler adjectives: epic, amazing, stunning, 8k, masterpiece.
  • Length: over ~120 words for a short clip.
  • Ending: no instruction for how the shot finishes.
